Raft item icons
===============

Source: https://raft.fandom.com/wiki/Items and each linked item page.
The Raft Wiki content is available under CC BY-SA unless otherwise noted.

Files named Game_<ID>.png were extracted from the user's local Raft installation
for this personal editor. Raft and its game artwork are property of Redbeet
Interactive/Axolot Games.

The icons and Catalog/items.metadata.txt can be refreshed with:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/Update-ItemCatalog.ps1
